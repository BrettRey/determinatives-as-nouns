## Argument architecture (as submitted)

- Two-stage: (i) headhood is established via partitive constructions; (ii) fusion is decoupled — `mine`/`Kim's` show fused Det--Head is compatible with noun heads, so fusion is no objection.
- Three discriminators support the reanalysis: paradigm productivity, structural saturation, internal modification.
- Frame: extends the AUX-into-V move (Pullum & Wilson 1977) to determinative-into-noun.
- Methodological foil: Payne, Huddleston & Pullum 2010 (same journal, distributional category-status argument).
