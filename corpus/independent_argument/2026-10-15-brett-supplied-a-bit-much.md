## Reading

The verb comes immediately before *a bit*, so no *not* or *n't* sits between them in any of these strings. Negation elsewhere in the sentence (*I don't think it's a bit much*) and questions (*would it be a bit much?*) can't be ruled out without the lines. Compared with the 69 lines of `[be] a little much .` (4 October), *a bit much* is the commoner way of grading *much*. It runs parallel to *CGEL*'s NP modifier *a bit lax* ([38iii], p. 549). The 4 October record (`2026-10-04-brett-supplied-modified-much.md`) called the *little* half untested; this list leaves that unchanged.
