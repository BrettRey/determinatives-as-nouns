*CGEL* already gives the construction, with *all* as an intensifying modifier of a phrase containing *the*: "All is also used as an intensifying modifier of AdjPs, AdvPs, or DPs that have *the* or *that* as modifier: *I feel [all the better for it]*" (p. 377), and "All, as in [37x], *all confused*, means 'completely'. In addition, it occurs, as in NP structure, before *the* and *that*: *I feel all the better for it* (suggesting a somewhat higher degree than *the better for it*)" (p. 549). Pages checked against the PDF.

Bearing: two determinatives, *all* and *the*, as degree modifiers in a predicative AdjP. That's the function §5.3 discusses. *All better* (Brett's *it's all better*) is a separate string and hasn't been searched.
