## Why this string

`[be] little .` (4 October) couldn't be used, because *little* meaning small swamps it and the tagger doesn't separate the senses. *Too little, too late* can't mean small, so it isolates *too little* in its quantity sense. The question for §2.2: does predicative *too little*, like *too much*, take subjects that aren't amounts?

## Caveat

It's an idiom, a coordination of *too little* and *too late* evaluated as a whole. A referee can say it doesn't show what *too little* does on its own. The answer is that its first coordinate gets the evaluative reading *too much* gets (*The makeup was too much*): the subject is an act judged insufficient, not a quantity.
