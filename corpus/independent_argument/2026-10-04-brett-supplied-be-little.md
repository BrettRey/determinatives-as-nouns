## Reading

Nearly every line is adjectival *little* of size or age: *when I was little*, *since we were little*, *they're little*, *She's little*. Those are the ordinary adjective and bear on nothing in §2.2.

Quantity *little* appears only in existentials, the use the article already cites from NOW:

- 118, *Denver Post* (NEWS, 2012): "Hats off to the University of Colorado for providing much-needed leadership where there has been little ."
- 154, bahai-library.com (WEB, 2012): "destiny and welfare of those who are to follow us. Otherwise there can be little ."
- 162, mercurynews blog (BLOG, 2012): "saying there's lots of demand for the channel, and DirecTV saying there 's little ."

Borderline, probably size: 133, *Vanity Fair* (WEB, 2012), "It felt good to let go, but that feeling was little . It was dwarfed by the recognition I had just allowed in."

No line on the page shows predicative quantity *little* with an entity subject (the frame of Solt's (76) *\*John's patience is little*), affirmative or negative. That is a page of an untaggable query, not a search: it neither supports nor tests the *little* half of the claim. A query that could test it would need a lexical frame that excludes the size reading, which this one can't.
