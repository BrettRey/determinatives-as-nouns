## What the hits are

- **Proper names and products.** Matt Every (PGA golfer), Henry Every (pirate), Bob Every, Suzuki Every, Rabobank's Every, and *The Every* (Eggers, 2021). The bulk of the sentence-initial cases.
- **Slips for *everyone* or *everything*, mostly informal.** *every was worried about hygiene*, *every was calling me*, *every has their own stereotypes*, *not every has a happy Christmas*, *every has conducted themselves fantastically*. Reader comments, quoted speech, and some lightly edited copy. These are performance errors of omission, not uses of independent *every*.
- **Typos for *ever*.** *if there every was one*, *like no one every was*.
- **The residue: *every* in the *each* reading, in edited prose, with an antecedent set.** Three clear cases:
  - *each car offers something unique to the market. Every has its strengths and will speak to its unique audience* (ZA, 2020-04-30)
  - *a special monument decorated with the city seal and the park name. Every has a story, many with themed play structures* (US, 2025-07-30)
  - *off pressure plans tailored to each opponent's pass protection rules. Every has different blocking mechanics* (GB, 2024-02-10)
  Borderline: *how kind and generous every was that attended* (GB, 2023-05-10), *every has a right to do what they feel is right* (NZ, 2017-08-04, quoted speech).

## What it means for the claim

A categorical exclusion is too strong as stated, and a referee with a corpus would say so. The paper's own framing already has the right slot: *every* is a restricted member, and the restriction is a matter of rate and reading, not absence. Independent *every* is attested in the *each*-like anaphoric reading at a very low rate in edited news prose, and much more often as an error for *everyone*. The generic subject use the manuscript stars (#*Every arrived*) is not attested in these lines at all: every residue case has an antecedent set. That distinction (anaphoric *each*-like use marginally attested; antecedent-free use not) is sharper than the current claim and better supported.
