## What it shows

Every line has a personal pronoun with the indefinite article, denoting the set of individuals fitting the pronoun's gender description: "Is it a he or a she?" asks whether it's male or female. That is the secondary set-denoting use of §6.1 (article, set denotation), which the article currently leaves open for pronouns ("whether the personal pronouns do (*the hes and shes*) I leave open").

- It occurs across registers: spoken news (CNN, CBS, PBS), TV, film, fiction, web, blog, newspaper.
- It extends past *he* and *she*: line 2, "Jamie didn't want to be a he or a she but a they"; line 21, "I always called her a she".
