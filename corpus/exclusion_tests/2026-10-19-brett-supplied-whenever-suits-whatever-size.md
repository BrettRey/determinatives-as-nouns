**Why it was run.** §7 of the structural paper supports two of its claims with constructed examples.
- *[Whenever suits you] is fine* applies subject relativization to the PP fused relatives. Muse's paragraph review (19 October) misread it as having no subject gap.
- *Buy [whatever size fits]* shows a fused relative where the relative word is only the determiner (against \!*Buy [any size fits]*).

**Result.**

| Line | Source | Reading |
|---|---|---|
| *Just let me know whenever suits you* | *McMafia*, 2018, TV (British) | *whenever* is the subject of *suits*. After *know*, a reader could take the clause as an embedded interrogative ("let me know when suits you") rather than a fused relative. |
| *bring it around some time … you know, whenever suits* | *Janet King*, 2017, TV (Australian; *this arvo* follows) | A PP fused relative as time adjunct, with intransitive *suits*; *whenever* is its subject |
| *cutting the sponge up into whatever size fits* | *Coral Sea Dreaming: Awaken*, 2010, documentary narration | An NP fused relative as object of *into*; *whatever size* is the subject of *fits*, and *whatever* is only its determiner |

**Bearing.** Both constructions are attested with a subject gap, though thinly and in speech or narration. The *whatever size fits* line is the paper's construction word for word, so the example can be attested rather than constructed. In the *whenever* lines, *suits* needs a subject, which only *whenever* can be. So on the head analysis each is a bare relative with a subject gap, which is what §7 claims. The interrogative reading of the *McMafia* line makes the *Janet King* line the cleaner case.

**Applied, 19 October.** §7 now quotes the *Coral Sea Dreaming* line, with a starred partner on its frame. The *whenever* example stays constructed but is now an adjunct (*Come round whenever suits you*), backed in kind by the two TV lines.
