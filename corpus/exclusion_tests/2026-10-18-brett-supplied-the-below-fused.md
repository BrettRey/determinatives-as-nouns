**Question.** CGEL says *below*, unlike *above*, "has no special discourse-location use: it belongs only to the preposition category" (Huddleston & Pullum 2002: 1555–1556), so there's no *below* counterpart of *Her conclusion is the same as [the above]*. The attributive use is in `2026-10-18-brett-supplied-the-below-noun.md`. Does *the below* also head a nominal on its own?

## Reading

*The below* heads a nominal for position in the text, as *the above* does, in edited magazines and journals as well as on the web, and once with a singular verb (*None of the below is meant*). With the attributive record, both of the uses CGEL gives for adjective *above* occur with *below*. *The below* also has a use CGEL doesn't give for *above*, for a place or world below, usually paired with *the above*.
