## Reading

After *the*, these three are mostly nouns with senses of their own: *the off* 'the start', *the under* 'the under bet', *the in* 'a way in'. They head a nominal in their spatial sense only in contrast with *outside* or *upper* (4 of 31 lines), and none refers to position in the text. Among these prepositions, the use of *the above* and *the below* for position in the text is distinctive, which fits CGEL's treating it apart (p. 1555), though for both words, not only *above*.
