Provenance: Brett ran three searches by hand and pasted the frequency lists (strings and counts only, no KWIC lines). The strings aren't in what was pasted; going by the output they were *the* + adjective tag + pronoun, roughly `the ADJ me`, `the ADJ you`, `the ADJ him` (Brett to confirm). The counts are unscreened: no lines have been read.

## the ADJ me (6 types, 656 tokens)

THE REAL ME 350 · THE OLD ME 129 · THE NEW ME 124 · THE BEST ME 20 · THE OTHER ME 19 · THE INNER ME 14

Probably close to clean. Accusative *me* can't be the subject of a following clause, which rules out the correlative and relative parses that swamp the *you* list. Known risk: *ME* as an abbreviation (Maine, myalgic encephalomyelitis), most likely under THE NEW ME. One display page of THE NEW ME would show this.

## the ADJ you (48 types, 5,727 tokens)

Mostly not the use. *You* is nominative as well as accusative, so the list is dominated by:

- comparative correlatives: THE LONGER YOU 732, THE CLOSER 331, THE FASTER 253, THE OLDER 248, THE HARDER 177, THE EARLIER 146, THE HIGHER 135, and most of the other comparatives (*the longer you wait*);
- superlatives and other adjectives with a relative clause: THE BEST YOU 1847 (*the best you can*), THE CLOSEST 122, THE WORST 79, THE LITTLE 17 (*the little you know*), THE GOOD 75 (probably *the good you do*), THE HURT 19 (*the hurt you caused*), THE OTHERS 49;
- prepositional idioms: THE SUDDEN 52 (*all the sudden you*), THE LEFT 37 (*on the left you*), THE OUTSIDE 21, THE MILITARY 32 (*in the military you*), THE BLUE 13 (*out of the blue you*).

Probably the use, mostly or entirely: THE REAL YOU 352, THE NEW YOU 105, THE OLD YOU 56, THE DIVINE YOU 14, THE ORIGINAL YOU 10. Mixed: THE OTHER YOU 102 (*the other you met*), THE ONLY YOU 13, and the self-help use of THE BEST YOU and THE HEALTHIER/HAPPIEST YOU sitting inside the superlative counts. None of these can go in the paper as counts without reading lines.

## the ADJ him (15 types, 63 tokens)

THE REAL HIM 38 · OLD 4 · OTHER 3 · NEW 3 · INDIVIDUAL 3 · BIGGEST 2 · TRUE 2 · YOUNGER, UNIVERSAL, OUTER, NOTORIOUS, INNER, FICTIONAL, COOK-HATED, ADOLESCENT 1 each

Probable non-uses: COOK-HATED (a clause, *the cook hated him*, mistagged), INDIVIDUAL (likely *the individual him- or herself*), BIGGEST (unclear), UNIVERSAL (possibly mention of generic *him*).

## Reading

*The real me* 350 and *the real you* 352 make the article-plus-adjective use of personal pronouns routine, not the wordplay the *mes* lines might suggest. Only the *me* list can be cited as a list of counts as it stands. For *you* and *him*, cite *the real you* and *the real him* after a sample of lines has been read.
