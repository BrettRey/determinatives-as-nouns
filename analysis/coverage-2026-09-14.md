## The two legs that carry least

**proper noun: no claims at all**, across all 11 constructions. The manuscript is not silent on proper nouns, but what it has is taxonomic statement plus three data points borrowed from *CGEL* (*Kim's preferences* L118, *the Smiths* L184, *Kim left* L370) and two restrictive property claims (L148, L734). Exactly one of the paper's eleven constructions has a proper-noun instance anywhere in the manuscript (*Kim's preferences* in Det function); the other ten have none. L726 concedes the point: extending the feature matrix to common nouns, proper nouns and adjectives "would require new sampling, feature selection, and coding across all groups."

**pronoun: 1 claim** (lex_my). The manuscript adds *poor old me* (L732), *She left* (L370), *only you* (modifier section) and the case/genitive discussion (L190), all CGEL-cited.

The determinative and common-noun legs carry 63 and 24 claims. The taxonomy is four-way; the evidence is close to two-way, with an adjectival contrast class (22 claims) doing more work than two of the four subcategories it is meant to sit beside.

## Constructions resting on a single subcategory

4 of 11 constructions have claims from at most one of the four coordinate subcategories:

- `peripheral_mod_admission` (7 claims): determinative only
  - **partly artifact**: Sec. `Modifier selection and attachment' gives *only you* (pronoun) and *Usually a careful driver, Anne found...* (common noun) alongside *almost every experienced teacher*. Manuscript covers 3/4; proper noun remains untested.
- `dependent_det` (3 claims): determinative only
  - **artifact**: L118 gives Det function for a pronoun (*my preferences*), a proper noun (*Kim's preferences*) and a common noun (*people's preferences*), citing CGEL 354-355, 470-471. The manuscript covers 4/4 here; the extraction simply did not select that passage.
- `comparative_complement` (1 claim): determinative only
  - **principled**: L164 groups comparative complementation with grade and degree-modifier selection as an *adjectival* property, weighed as a counterweight. Four-way nominal coverage is not expected and its absence is not a gap.
- `dependent_internal_mod` (1 claim): none of the four only
  - **principled**: This is the adjectival control construction of Sec. 2.3 (*numerous people* against *many people*). It exists to contrast with the nominal diagnostics, not to be one.

`artifact` means the manuscript covers what the claim set does not, so the gap is in the extraction. `principled` means four-way coverage is not expected there. Neither is a defect in the paper.
