# Pairwise review: §3 (Restricted members and the scope of the grouping) × §6 (Coordinate or nested subcategories)

§3 argues that items lacking independent uses (*the*, *a*, *every*) and the *no*/*none* paradigm belong inside determinative, and so inside Noun, via systemic integration and a pronoun-paradigm precedent; §6 argues that determinative sits coordinate with pronoun rather than nested inside it. They answer two different scope questions (outer boundary of determinative; its rank within Noun) and both lean on pronoun–determinative parallels, which is where the friction lies.

## Findings

**1. QUALIFIED CONCERN — §3's *my*/*mine* precedent is a pronoun–determinative parallel that §6's inventory of nesting grounds never assesses.**
§3 l.326 ("supplies a precedent for restricted membership within Noun … one pronoun paradigm"); l.346 ("Form selection remains necessary, just as with *my*/*mine*"). §6 l.804 ("a determiner is a pronoun whose valency permits the relevant common-noun dependent"); l.814–818 (candidates tested: descriptive content, context dependence, pro-form gender, determination/modification restrictions); l.820 ("person, case, and reflexivity organize much of the pronoun system").
A genitive pronoun licensing a singular count noun and excluding *the* is precisely what Hudson's definition at l.804 covers, so the paper's own bridge between a pronoun paradigm and a determinative paradigm is the nesting theorist's paradigm case. §6 tests four other shared properties but not this one, and its closing division of labour assigns case to pronouns without saying whether *no*/*none* instantiates the same generalization or a different one. No contradiction: §3 places the precedent "within Noun", matching §6 l.820. But the most natural objection to §6's conclusion is supplied by §3 and left unanswered.
Repair: one or two sentences after l.818 — e.g., that *my*/*mine* is a case contrast within a genitive paradigm whereas CGEL's *no*/*none* (cited §3 l.346) is not, so the shared fact is form selection by construction, recorded at paradigm level; and that *my*'s Det role is a function-level fact the coordinate analysis need not lift to category. Alternatively soften §3 l.346 from "just as" to "as a precedent".

**2. QUALIFIED CONCERN — the extension of "determinative" shifts at §6 l.802, and the weighting of the inventory inverts between the sections.**
§3 l.328–332 (the system includes *the*, *a*, *every* and complex determinatives built on *few*, *little*, *many*). §6 l.802 ("Hudson instead puts determinative inside pronoun. Both group determinatives with nouns"); l.806 ("sets aside *all*, cardinal numerals, and quantifiers restricted to plural or non-count nouns … remains unsettled").
L.802 uses "determinative" for Hudson's class; l.806 then shows his class is narrower than the inventory §3 assumes. Further, Hudson's core criteria (singular-count licensing, mutual exclusion) select exactly the items §3 must argue *into* the grouping, while setting aside the independently used items (*few*, *little*, *many*, numerals) that carry §3's *a few*/*many a* evidence and §2's category-level argument. L.806 partly self-corrects, but "Both group determinatives with nouns" overstates the overlap and the inversion stays implicit.
Repair: write "his determiner class" at l.802; add a clause at l.806 noting that his criteria privilege the members §3 treats as restricted while leaving unsettled those whose independent uses ground Noun membership.

**3. CONTEXT CHECK — reynolds2021 and the restricted articles.** §6 l.810 says clusters "broadly resemble the pronoun and determinative inventories". Whether *the*, *a*, *every* were among the 138 forms, and where they fell, bears directly on §3 l.326–338 but isn't stated. Needs the source (and possibly §2). If they clustered with determinatives, a one-line cross-reference from §3 is cheap corroboration; if excluded or outliers, §3 should say so.

**4. CONTEXT CHECK (minor) — terminology.** "the D-noun analysis" (§3 l.340) vs "the proposed hierarchy"/"the coordinate analysis" (§6 l.802, 808). Check §1–2 define "D-noun"; if so, no action.

- **Level discipline.** §3 l.326 locates the precedent "within Noun"; §6 l.820 puts "shared nominal structure … at the Noun level". Compatible.
- **Hudson's category constancy** (§6 l.804) matches §3 l.340's claim that Hudson preserves category continuity.
- **Degree modification** as §3's link for *the* (l.330) matches §6 l.820 listing "degree" among determinative-organizing dimensions.
- **Hedging is parallel** and matches the abstract: §3 l.338 "retain … because these connections preserve a broader system"; §6 l.820–822 "Nesting remains a coherent alternative … leaves this further choice open".
- **Promise routing.** §3 l.348 hands the structural account to "the following section" (§4); §6 owes nothing here.
- **Footnote arithmetic** at §6 l.810: 232 − 155 = 77 = 76 + 1; the "isn't authenticated" hedge is appropriate.
- **No wasteful repetition.** The two uses of "little descriptive content" (§3 l.338; §6 l.814) target different boundaries.
