# Pairwise review: §3 (Restricted members and the scope of the grouping) – §7 (Conclusion)

§3 argues that articles and *every*, lacking independent argument use, still belong to the Noun grouping derivatively via the determinative system (against Spinillo's article trio) and that *no*/*none* is a form-level rather than member-level restriction; §7 restates the package, with line 828 serving as the direct recap of §3, so the relationship is source-to-summary.

## Findings

**1. QUALIFIED CONCERN (with CONTEXT CHECK on §4/§5): "can't stand independently" vs. correlative *the* as a bare NP under the package.**
§3 320 "Articles lack ordinary independent uses"; 324 "if they can't stand independently"; 330 "*the bigger the better* … modifies comparative AdjPs, as other determinatives do in *much bigger* or *no better* … preserving its exclusion from independent argument use." §7 830 "Ordinary Head gives bare … determinatives the familiar Nom–NP structure"; 832 "Nominal degree modifiers already occur in attributive AdjPs, so their distribution doesn't establish an added cost of NP projection." Line 832 commits the package to degree-modifier determinatives (*much* in *much bigger*) projecting NPs; 330 puts correlative *the* in that same class. By parity, *the* in *the bigger* is a Head-alone NP — a bare, non-Det occurrence of the article. That is compatible with 330's precise wording ("argument use") but not with the unqualified framing at 320/324, and 828's "needn't independently exhibit the whole profile" is stated at the looser level. A linguist will test article membership exactly here ("can *the* ever head an NP?") and gets two answers depending on which line they read. §3 is right not to use the NP status of correlative *the* as evidence for nounhood (it follows from the analysis; using it would be circular), but should say so. Repair: write "independent argument use" at 320 and 324; confirm in §4/§5 that correlative *the* is either analysed as Head-alone NP or explicitly exempted, and register whichever holds in §3.

**2. QUALIFIED CONCERN: the *my*/*mine* precedent operates at a different level from the article case.**
§3 326 "*CGEL*'s treatment of *my* supplies a precedent for restricted membership within Noun … Dependent *my* and independent *mine* belong to one pronoun paradigm … For articles, the corresponding positive evidence is integration into determinative"; 346 "just as with *my*/*mine*"; 348 distinguishes "members' permitted constructions" from "the forms selected within a paradigm." §7 828 "Articles needn't independently exhibit the whole profile, and *no*/*none* retains its form-selection conditions." The precedent is form-level (a restricted form inside a lexeme with unrestricted forms), which fits *no*/*none* exactly but not *the*/*a*, where no form has independent argument use. §3 pivots to system integration without stating that the precedent doesn't reach the article case; §7's two-level summary is cleaner than the argument it summarizes. No contradiction, but a visible gap in the paper's most exposed step. Repair: one sentence at 326 saying the precedent shows only that non-independence doesn't bar Noun membership, with the lexeme-level extension resting on 338. If a lexeme-level precedent is wanted, CGEL's pronoun treatment of distribution-restricted existential *there* may serve — author to verify.

**3. CONTEXT CHECK (§4/§5): the mechanism that keeps *the*/*a* out of argument-function NPs.**
§3 338 "without granting unrestricted use to any member"; 348 "A successful structural account has to respect both." §7 832 "The package retains lexical and constructional restrictions." Between these sections the block on **The arrived* is asserted, not shown. Not a defect if §4/§5 state the lexical restriction and note parity with CGEL's own stipulation that articles can't be fused determiner-heads; parity of stipulation is what makes 832's "no added cost" hold for this class.

**4. QUALIFIED CONCERN (low): two terms shift referent across the pair.**
"Degree modification" at 330 ("*The* also participates in degree modification") means functioning as a modifier; the abstract's "degree modification" (0029) means being modified, and §7 uses "degree uses" (826) and "Nominal degree modifiers" (832). "Profile" at 328 is the determinative profile; at 828 "the whole profile" is the nominal profile of 826. Repair: "functions as a degree modifier" at 330; "the whole nominal profile" at 828.

**5. Linguistic judgment, not textual contradiction.** Line 330 assumes correlative *the* is the same lexeme as the article. If the cited CGEL pages treat them as distinct, the integration evidence at 330 (restated implicitly by 828) is weaker than presented. Worth a clause acknowledging the assumption.
