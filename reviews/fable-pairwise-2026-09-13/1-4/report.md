- Why it matters: l.50's second direction is the only place the structural half feeds back into the categorial half; as §4 stands, the reader sees rows 1 and 2 as equivalent.
- Repair: one or two sentences at l.418–420 naming the two fit points (single Head-licensing category; single Det-filler type), and either relabel the fusion column as taxonomy-neutral or say why row 4 isn't live.
