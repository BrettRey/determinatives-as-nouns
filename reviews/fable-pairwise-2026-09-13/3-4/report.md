# Pair review — §3 *Restricted members and the scope of the grouping* × §4 *Ordinary Head, fusion, and modifier attachment*

§3 fixes the *scope* of the Noun grouping (Det-only `the`/`a`/`every` retained; member restrictions separated from `no`/`none`, `my`/`mine` form selection), and §4 compares ordinary Head with fusion for members that do occur independently; §3 348 hands §4 the requirement that a structural account respect both distinctions.

## 1. CONTEXT CHECK — §3's structural requirement is deferred through §4 to §5
**Anchors.** §3 348: "A successful structural account has to respect both." §4 352 "preserves the relevant restrictions"; 418 "subject to their restrictions"; 420, 526, 612 defer the explicit statement to §5 (sec:det-uniform, sec:costs).
**Gap.** §4's displays give restricted members full NPs of the argument phrase type — `the` as Det:NP_D (485), `every` as N_D→Nom→NP_D (546–548) — and 420 commits determinative heads to NP phrase type generally. Excluding `*The left` therefore needs a lexeme-level restriction on which *functions* an NP_D may realize, distinct from the inflectional mechanism used for `no`/`none` and `my`/`mine` (§3 346, §4 608). §4 never names it, and §3 330 adds a further function to license (`the` as degree Mod in `the bigger the better`).
**Why it matters.** This is the cost §3 says the package must absorb; if §5 encodes only form selection, the articles (no form alternation) fall through.
**Check.** Verify that §5 states (a) member restrictions as function restrictions on N_D-headed NPs, (b) `the`'s AdjP-Mod use, (c) partitive selection of the independent form (§3 346). If absent, one sentence at §4 418 suffices.

## 2. QUALIFIED CONCERN — the `my` precedent is form-level, as the pair itself classifies it
**Anchors.** §3 326: `my` "supplies a precedent for restricted membership within Noun." §3 346 ("just as with my/mine"), §3 348, and §4 608 ("The my/mine alternation remains a form-selection condition, parallel to no/none") place the paradigm on the *form* side of 348's distinction; §4 608 gives `mine` unrestricted ordinary Head.
**Issue.** `my` shows a noun *form* can be confined to Det; the articles are *lexemes* with no independent form (§3 320). §3 326 half-concedes ("their nounhood depends on the category-level argument"), but the headline sentence overstates. Evidential-weight judgment, not a textual contradiction.
**Repair.** Rephrase 326 to "a precedent for a noun form confined to Det", noting that the articles' lack of an independent form is why the argument rests on integration (328–332).

## 3. QUALIFIED CONCERN — `a few` is left between two analyses
**Anchors.** §3 332: `a` "enters the complex determinatives a few, a little" (fixed units). §4 474: "few heads an ordinary NP both alone and in the few, where the fills Det", "the same Head and dependent functions as the lucky survivors."
**Issue.** §4's `the few` machinery invites Det + Head for `a few`; §3's selectional statement for `a` (328, "a singular count target") in fact blocks that parallel and supports the fixed reading — but the inference is left to the reader, and §4 is silent on `a few` while analysing `the few`.
**Why it matters.** §3's evidence for `a`'s integration changes character with the analysis chosen.
**Repair.** One clause at §3 332 or §4 474: `a few` is fixed because `a`'s count selection excludes the `the few` parallel.

## 4. QUALIFIED CONCERN — `every` + `almost` is recast by §4.4
**Anchors.** §3 336–338: "every's modifier permissions cross the proposed article boundary." §4 534: "Approximative and focusing modifiers have established NP parallels; the degree series selects the four gradable quantifiers"; 536 `for almost my entire life`; fig:every attaches `almost` peripherally.
**Issue.** On §4's own split, `almost` is the NP-general kind, not the determinative-specific degree kind. The contrast with `*almost the/a` survives, but as selection (`almost` with `all`/`each`/`any`/`no`), not a permission peculiar to the determinative system. §4 596 ("premodifier permissions from its determinative base") keeps a lexical-permission framing, so this is emphasis, not contradiction.
**Repair.** Say "selectional" at 336 and point to §4.4; extend 348's handoff ("Head relations") to include modifier attachment, which §4.4–4.5 cover.

## Checks that pass
- **No circularity:** §4 418 depends on §3's restrictions; §3 330/338 don't presuppose ordinary Head. The abstract's "neither choice requires the other" holds in this pair.
- **Partitives:** §3 346 (independent form required) coheres with §4 455–457 (partitive quantifier as Head with PP Comp) and with §3 338's grouping of partitive alongside non-Det uses.
- **Continuity:** §3 340 ("CGEL, Hudson, and the D-noun analysis can all preserve it") and §4 556 ("preserves that continuity") agree; §4 doesn't claim exclusivity.
- **Structural cash-out:** fig:genitive (426–428) and fig:few left (485) realize §3's `my`/article analogy as noun-headed NPs in Det; an optional cross-reference at §4 420 would make it explicit.
- **Repetition:** §4 608's `no`/`none` clause is orienting recapitulation, not duplication; the sections don't repeat each other's arguments.

No epistemic, authorization, or coordination risks were evident in this packet.
