# Pairwise review: §4 *Ordinary Head, fusion, and modifier attachment* × §6 *Coordinate or nested subcategories*

§4 argues that, once determinatives are inside Noun, ordinary Head (not CGEL fusion) fits independent, partitive, externally determined, compound, and genitive constructions, with modifiers split between NP-periphery and Nom; §6 asks where inside Noun determinatives sit (coordinate with pronoun vs nested under it) and asserts that this choice is orthogonal to §4's structures (0822). §6 presupposes §4's inheritance-licensed trees and claims path-neutrality; §4 in turn supplies several pronoun–determinative parallels that §6 must weigh.

## Findings

**1. QUALIFIED CONCERN (+ CONTEXT CHECK: Appendix A, §1) — Hudson's headedness vs §4's Head relations.**
§6 0802 presents the two hierarchies as differing only in "whether pronouns and determinatives form an intermediate category." But 0804 reports Hudson's determiner as "a pronoun whose valency permits the relevant common-noun *dependent*": the common noun depends on the determinative. §4 fixes the reverse: "The dependent expression still has *apples* as its ultimate head" (0358); "That smaller NP functions as Det in the larger NP, whose ultimate head is *teacher*" (0538; fig:some 0397–0401). §6 0822 then says "Both inheritance paths supply the fragment's structures." That holds only for nesting detached from Hudson's headedness, which §6 never states; 0804 also applies the paper's label "dependent use" (§4 0415) to a structure in which the determinative is the head. Matters because §6's "Nesting remains a coherent alternative" (0820) reads as compatible with §4, yet the coherent alternative isn't Hudson's as described. Repair: one clause at 0802/0804 ("abstracting from Hudson's treatment of the determiner as head, which the fragment's Head relations reverse"), or a pointer to Appendix A if it already makes that abstraction — parent to check.

**2. QUALIFIED CONCERN — §4's *my/mine* ~ *no/none* parallel is unassessed in §6.**
§4 0608: "The *my*/*mine* alternation remains a form-selection condition, parallel to *no*/*none*"; 0420 groups *Kim's* and *my* as Det genitives, and fig:genitive treats *Kim's* as invariant. A dependent/independent form alternation is thus attested for pronoun genitives and a determinative but not for common or proper nouns — precisely the kind of "grammatical generalizations applying to pronouns and determinatives together" §6 0808 says would motivate the intermediate level. §6 0814–0818 canvasses context-dependence, pro-form gender, and determination/modification restrictions, then concludes "the comparisons here don't establish a further shared set of grammatical rules" (0820). Linguistic judgment: the alternation is lexically listed and tiny, so it likely doesn't warrant a level — but §6 should say so, or §4 should weaken "parallel to" to "comparable to." One sentence at 0814–0818 suffices.

**3. QUALIFIED CONCERN (minor) — "adverbial modifier patterns" omits §4's peripheral/internal split.**
§6 0818: "Their adverbial modifier patterns differ from the restricted adjectival pattern of personal pronouns." §4 0536 lists *only you* — a personal pronoun with a peripheral adverb — alongside *for almost my entire life*, and 0534 explicitly separates "NP-peripheral modification from modification inside Nom"; 0564 pins the determinative-specific permissions as *internal* (degree series on four quantifiers; approximatives on externally determined cardinals). Read against §4, peripheral adverbial modification is NP-general, so 0818 is true but underspecified and weaker than §4 allows. Repair: "internal adverbial (degree and approximative) patterns (§\ref{sec:payne})".

## Checks that pass

- **Proper-noun control used consistently.** §4 0447 pairs *She left* with *Kim left*; §6 0814, 0816 use proper names to block the nesting inference. Same move, no drift.
- **Non-uniformity of external determination.** §4 0474 (*the few*) vs §4 0596 ("The compound construction independently excludes external determination") supplies both halves of §6 0818's claim, and compounds pattern with §6 0816's pronouns — §4's data cut exactly as §6 needs.
- **Ordinary Head as a Noun-level property.** §4 0418 ("inheritance … subject to their restrictions"), 0608 ("extends ordinary headedness to an existing noun subcategory") ↔ §6 0820, 0822. No circularity: §4 doesn't invoke §6's coordinate structure; §6 doesn't license §4's trees.
- **Recapitulation.** §6 0818 re-cites *the few*, *these few here* (CGEL 415–416) from §4 0474–0476 for a different purpose (non-uniformity, not fusion); brief and helpful. A cross-reference to §\ref{sec:modification} could replace the re-citation but isn't required.
- **Promises.** Both sections defer constraints to the §5 fragment (§4 0506, 0526, 0612; §6 0822). Nothing promised between the pair is undischarged. §4 uses "inheritance" (\term, 0418) before §6 (`sec:inheritance`) does; ordering is fine — CONTEXT CHECK only whether §2 gives the primary definition.
