## Findings and action

All six named reviews recommended revision of the reviewed draft. Their strongest shared observation was concrete: the draft deferred the matched grammar comparison it needed. A new fragment now states the shared lexical permissions, varies nominal classification separately from fusion, and shows where direct and nested inheritance remain equivalent. The [bounded Pullum follow-up](pullum-followup.md) found the additions coherent and requested two clarifications, both applied: permissions follow the relevant head projection, and argument permission remains subject to case restrictions.

The initial draft omitted the closest dissolution alternatives and the developed fusion account. The revision restores source-grounded discussion of Spinillo 2004, Van Eynde 2003 and Payne et al. 2007, explicitly retains adjectival fusion, and identifies compound modification as an extension the simple fragment does not complete. The abstract now leads with the positive analysis. Article licensing is stated explicitly.

The [empirical auditor](corpus.md) reran all matrix and corpus analyses, obtained byte-identical outputs, independently reconstructed the DISCO components, and read all 65 Det–Head contexts. A [different-model numerical check](numbers-second-model.md), using Sol, also found no discrepancies. Euclidean distance, equal feature weighting and the permutation minimum are now explicit.

## Disagreements and limits

The GLM typological reviews demanded broader cross-linguistic treatment; that recommendation was not adopted. The paper is explicitly about English lexical categories, and a universal analysis of definiteness markers would change its scope. The clean-room reader also repeated the missing-comparison objection, but incorrectly grouped Payne with opponents of a separate D category and questioned grammatical plural agreement with *none*. Those claims were not accepted.

The board’s agreement about missing exposition does not establish that one hierarchy is correct. Its recommendations to show unique superiority over every nominal alternative exceed the bounded descriptive claim now made. The final revised draft has not received a second full board, nor does it need one before Brett reads it.

The 155/232-feature discrepancy in the earlier publication remains a separate author decision about updating the old data description. No contact with the venue or repository update was made.

Brett flagged overcomplication after requesting that Astra be named in the acknowledgements. Further expansion stopped. The appropriate next step is his reading of the working draft.
