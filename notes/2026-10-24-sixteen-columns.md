# Sixteen columns whose definitions departed from the 2021 senses

The mismatch figures come from wave 2's calibration: eight pronoun and determinative forms coded blind by Codex and Muse, against Brett's 2021 cells. A column is listed if a quarter or more of its cells mismatched. "Sure" and "inferred" refer to how confident the codebook's definition was. Six columns need Brett's answer (marked **Question** or **Unclear**); the rest can be fixed in the definitions. In many cases, I think the answer is to add columns.

| Column | Definition the coders saw | What the 2021 y's suggest | Fix or question |
| - | - | - | - |
| **pro-form** (7/16; inferred) | It is a pro-form: it stands for a phrase recoverable from context. | y for every pronoun form, interrogative *who* and dummy *it* included; n for every determinative. | **Settled, 24 Oct:** the 2021 coding was wrong (Brett). Recode on a stated definition. *CGEL* p. 1462: "interrogative *who* is no pro-form". |
| **genitive form in the paradigm** (5/16; inferred) | Its paradigm has a genitive form. | Pronouns only; compounds (*anybody's*) and nouns (*dog's*) n. | "A genitive form other than the plain form plus *'s* (*I/my*, *he/his*, *who/whose*)." That fits every 2021 cell. This is a morphological column, and I don’t take ’s to count morphologically. That said, there should be a distinct column to distinguish those that take ’s from those that don’t. |
| **definite** (6/15; sure) | It can mark or have definite reference. | *the*, the demonstratives, *all*, *both*, personal pronouns y; *each*, *few*, interrogative *which* n. | "Makes the NP definite" (a classification), not "can occur in a definite NP". Should probably have columns for both of these. |
| **non-count amount** (4/15; sure) | It can quantify over or denote a non-count amount. | *the* and *which* n. | "Itself quantifies or measures a non-count amount (*much*, *a little*)." *The* doesn't quantify. Again, probably have columns for both. |
| ***not*** (11/16; sure) | *Not* can modify it or the NP it heads (*not many*, *not everyone*). | *not many*, *not everyone*, *not a few* y; *each*, *few*, *someone*, *the*, *she* n. | "*Not* combines with it to form a negated quantity," not constituent negation (*not the teacher but…*). **Question:** why are *his*, *her*, and *hers* y?  
I’m not sure; is there some discussion in CGEL? |
| ***at least*** (8/16; sure) | *At least* can modify it or the NP it heads. | Pronouns y (*at least him*), *some* y, *someone* n, *each* and *few* n. | **Unclear.** Quantity bound (*at least two*) or focus (*at least he*)? *Someone* n looks odd on either reading. I’m not sure; is there some discussion in CGEL? |
| ***quite*** (5/16; sure) | *Quite* can modify it or the NP it heads (*quite a few*, *quite everything*). | *every*, the compounds, *this* y; *the* n. | **Question:** was *quite the* (*quite the scholar*) excluded on purpose? In *quite the scholar*, we have *quite *and *the scholar *as siblings, and *the scholar *isn’t headed by *the*. Does this help with the other’s above? |
| **degree modifier of an AdjP** (4/16; sure) | It (or its NP) can modify an adjective phrase (*much bigger*, *that big*, *a lot bigger*). | *the* y (*the sooner the better*), *all* y (*all the better*). | Add those as anchors; the coders didn't know Brett's analysis of *the*. Dialectal *some better* goes in the variety field. |
| **subject of a gerund-participial** (4/15; sure) | It (or its NP) can be the subject of a gerund-participial clause (*him joining you*). | *she* n. | "In a complement clause (*I don't mind him joining you*); absolutes (*She being ill, …*) don't count." |
| **measure genitive** (9/15; inferred) | It can be the determiner of the genitive in a measure phrase (*a few days' time*). | Indefinite and quantity determiners only (*a*, *a few*, *many*, *several*, *two*); *the*, *this*, *each*, *which* n. | "Determines the plural measure noun in *in \_\_ days' time*." Definite genitives (*the day's events*) don't count. |
| **coordination with independent genitives** (7/15; inferred) | It can be coordinated with an independent genitive (*this and mine*). | Demonstratives and personal pronouns y; the quantifiers n. | **Unclear.** Parallel definite reference (*this and mine*, *him and his*)? Brett to define.  
I don’t understand what’s unclear. |
| **coordination with dependent genitives** (7/15; inferred) | It can be coordinated with a dependent genitive before a noun (*my and your friends*). | The dependent genitives and *which*/*whichever* only. | Same definition, with *whose and which books* as an anchor; the coders let in *each and my*. |
| **coordination with other determiners** (4/15; inferred) | It can be coordinated with another determiner before a shared noun (*this and every day*). | *few*, *some*, *the* y. | Add anchors (*few or no friends*, *one or two days*). **Question:** what is *the* coordinated with? |
| ***else*/*more* after it** (4/15; inferred) | *Else* or *more* can follow it (*someone else*, *nothing more*). | *some* and *few* n, though *some more* and *a few more* are common. | Split into an *else* column and a *more* column. |
| **attributive modifier in a nominal** (4/16; inferred) | It can be an attributive modifier in a nominal (*the two books*). | *every* (*his every move*), *once* (*the once king*), *me* (*the me generation*), *each* y. | **Question:** what separates this from the pre-head-modifier column? If it's "with or without a determiner before the nominal", say so.  
Not sure. *The very few *would have a pre-head non-attributive modifier in the nominal. |
| **deictic** (4/16; inferred) | Its interpretation can depend on the speech situation (*this*, *here and now*, *the speaker*). | Speaker, addressee, *this*, *next* y; *she* and *the* n; interrogative *which* y. | "Speaker, addressee, or time or place of speaking"; third-person pronouns used with pointing don't count. **Question:** why is *which* y?  
Interrogative should be n but relative y? |
