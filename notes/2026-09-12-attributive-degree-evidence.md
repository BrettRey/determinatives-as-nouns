## What changes in the argument
CGEL explicitly treats degree _lots_ and _heaps_ as quantificational NPs (pp. 549–550). Its later exclusion of pre-head NP modifiers from attributive AdjPs (pp. 551–552) therefore conflicts with the straightforward analysis of _some heaps better photo's_. The _lots better_ {==_ones_==}{>>ones is a noun here for CGEL<<}{id="c1" by="user" at="2026-09-12T17:17:56.677Z"} excerpt supplies a parallel with anaphoric _one_. Both have an outer _some_, so CGEL's exceptional article-loss analysis of _a lot better player_ does not account for them.

The _miles_ examples broaden the comparison, but their own category and internal structure still need justification. The hyphen in _miles-better_ supplies no independent reason to insist on a compound analysis, and its absence in _miles better_ supplies no independent proof of phrasal structure. The clearest immediate counterexample uses _heaps_, whose NP analysis CGEL already supplies.

The evidence establishes attested attributive use, not unrestricted acceptability for every nominal degree expression. It leaves CGEL's judgment on {==_some a great deal better proposals_==}{>>the obvious clash here is the some a string<<}{id="c2" by="user" at="2026-09-12T17:18:48.419Z"} intact as a judgment about that expression. Lexical, constructional, and register differences need to be distinguished before the contrast can support a general category restriction.
## Proposed manuscript adjustment
In §5.3, lead from existing nominal degree modification to the attested attributive examples, then retain the more restricted _a great deal_ contrast. Replace the categorical advantage currently assigned to DP with an unresolved comparison of how each grammar expresses the actual restrictions. Carry that change into the introduction, Table 5, and the conclusion, where the same supposed cost is currently summarized.

This strengthens the comparison with quantificational common nouns. It does not by itself establish Noun membership for determinatives or decide between ordinary and fused Head. The approved adjustment is now applied to the manuscript; the two review comments are addressed below.
