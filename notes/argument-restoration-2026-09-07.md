# Argument restoration, 7 September 2026

The September rebuild removed substantive support for the proposal. This repair restores the argument against the April 29 draft (`1f9973b`) and the later working draft, while retaining the source and empirical corrections. The pre-repair source, PDF, local bibliography, exact April source and checksums are preserved in `snapshots/2026-09-07-argument-restoration/`.

## Diagnosis and manuscript contract

The principal failure was treating Noun as a proposed new superclass. CGEL already includes common nouns, proper nouns and pronouns. Removing their comparison obscured the standard against which determinatives should be assessed and made existing subcategory restrictions look like new costs. The shortened evidence section also retained qualifications without adequately explaining the combined positive inference. Several replies to alternatives disappeared.

The repaired paper argues for determinative as a fourth, coordinate subcategory within that existing Noun. It preserves English scope, the category/function distinction, N-headed NPs, the local determinative/pronoun distinction and the empirical record. Ordinary headedness is a further structural proposal. The comparison with Hudson still requires an argument about taxonomic nesting; distributional separation alone does not decide it.

This was an argumentative reconstruction, not a further shortening pass. The reader can now recover the sequence: establish the existing nounhood standard; show that all three admitted subcategories already supply determiners; compare independent determinatives and their internal structures; weigh their combined support; distinguish shared structure from retained restrictions and actual additional costs.

## Corrections deliberately retained

Hudson remains a nested nominal alternative with a different lexical inventory, not an analysis that leaves determiners outside Noun. The older nearby-proposals table is not restored because it conflated taxonomy, dependency and phrase headedness; the present taxonomy table and prose retain those distinctions. Anderson, Bruening, Déchaine and Wiltschko, Palmer, both Lyons sources and Sommerstein remain in the paper.

The unstable clustering result is not restored as a robust 93% confirmation, and the public 155-feature file is not silently treated as the reported 232-feature input. The gender comparison retains the correct source and does not turn *we/you* into determinatives or make gender prove nounhood. Historical article origins do not establish synchronic category membership. The earlier decision to keep the Lean supplement outside the article remains; the printed grammatical fragment provides the explicit comparison without a claim that formal consistency establishes the English facts.

| Scope and field-relativity | GREEN | Synchronic English and a limited fragment remain explicit; cardinal consequences do not extend to all numerals or languages. |
| Prospective revision | YELLOW | Rival-favouring conditions are stated; no prospective validation is claimed. Article restrictions are explicit before the fragment's derivations. |

The payoff is structural and comparative. The evidence establishes recurrent grammatical patterns, without a causal or homeostatic account. A future claim of independent predictive superiority would require specified unseen constructions or other outcomes and a comparison fixed before evaluating them. No such results were invented to strengthen this repair. The structural repair required here was to restore the existing standard and the positive comparative argument, not to add unsupported predictions.
