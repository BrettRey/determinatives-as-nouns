# Correcting the analysis of *a few*

Brett's correction: in *a few mistakes*, *few* supplies the determiner and *a* is a peripheral modifier. The September 17 revision instead made *a* the determiner of an inner nominal headed by *few*. That is the analysis to remove.

## Proposed replacement

In *a few mistakes*, *few* heads the phrase in determiner function, and *a* is a peripheral modifier of the NP headed by *mistakes*. The same peripheral *a* occurs with independent *few* in *a few arrived*. This use of *a* is distinct from its determiner use: its singular-count target restriction applies when it determines a nominal, not when it modifies an NP at the periphery.

The parallel expressions *a little* and *a great many* receive the same treatment: *little* and *many* supply the determiner before another nominal and the head in independent uses; *a* is peripheral. The permitted combinations and internal modifiers remain lexically restricted, including the obligatory modifier in *a great many*.

Internal *very* in *a very few mistakes* modifies *few*. Peripheral *quite* in *quite a few mistakes* precedes the peripheral *a*. The modifier orders remain facts to state; they no longer follow from treating *a few* as an ordinary internally determined NP. *The few mistakes* retains *the* as determiner and *few* as internal modifier. *Many a*, *such a*, and *what a* retain their separate predeterminer analysis.
