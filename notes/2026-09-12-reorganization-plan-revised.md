## Governing decision

Organize the paper around the conclusions its comparisons support. Bring the strongest nominal and adjectival connections into direct comparison, complete the intended category’s membership argument, then assess internal structure and its costs. Address coordinate versus nested rank as a further decision after that comparison.

The latest assessment improves on the earlier strength-first proposal. Quantificational common nouns provide a specific positive comparison, but their restricted distribution deserves the same scrutiny as the localized adjectival cluster. Breadth, recurrence, and grammatical integration apply to both. Localization alone neither defeats the adjectival evidence nor vindicates the nominal evidence.

The broader Noun argument remains a weighted profile comparison. Ordinary Head, shared NP determiner realization, and reuse of peripheral-modification grammar are available to both ordinary-Head taxonomies. The fragment audits their coverage and commitments; it does not supply independent predictive confirmation from judgments fixed as its inputs. These roles should be visible when each comparison first appears.

This remains an organizational clarity repair using the present evidence and substantive commitments. It does not assume an analysis-neutral inventory of facts: it distinguishes the contribution made by an observation, an interpretation of that observation, and a consequence of adopting that interpretation.

## Conducting the profile comparison

Independent distribution establishes the breadth of the phenomenon. It is not another confirmation merely because syntactic completeness is discussed separately. Present the relevant constructional range early in §2, including its restrictions; reserve the fuller discussion of contextual recovery and silent heads for §4.

Partitive complementation and number transparency belong in the common-noun comparison. Locating the overt quantifier’s Head role and comparing its representation with fusion belong in §4. Refer back to the examples when the argumentative job changes instead of repeating the entire comparison.

Modifier selection and ordering characterize the profiles: *very few* differs from *almost every*, and *a very few* differs from *quite a few*. The fuller proposed Nom/NP attachment analysis belongs in §4. Existing analyses can be cited in §2 where they supply evidence, but determinatives’ proposed peripheral NP attachment must not enter as an independently established reason for granting them NP projection.

The degree series, grade, and comparative complementation form the concentrated adjectival profile. Predicative use extends to *several*, and completeness modifiers have other selectional ranges; preserve those qualifications. The revised weighting paragraph should explain why the existing cross-constructional connections support retaining the gradable quantifiers within determinative. Merely calling their adjectival profile localized does not answer a redistribution proposal. If the existing material cannot support that transition, identify the gap rather than concealing it through order or emphasis.

Keep Table 2 near the end as a synoptic comparison. Distinguish observed permissions from descriptions dependent on the proposed attachment analysis, particularly the determinative entry for peripheral AdvP modification. Its rows should follow the exposition without implying an additive score.
