## The attachment correction

Your correction is right. The outer determiner in *the very few* does not establish that *very* is internal to the smaller NP headed by *few*. Both supplied assessments repeat my mistaken claim that the example supplies a control proving internal attachment. That claim is withdrawn.

Section 3.4 now gives the embedded analysis explicitly: in *the very few people*, the NP *very few* functions as Mod, and *very* can be peripheral to the inner NP headed by *few*. In independent *the very few who objected*, the inner NP can realize Mod–Head fusion. An ordinary-Head analysis with internal attachment remains an alternative; nounhood alone does not choose between them. Figure 3 now illustrates peripheral attachment for *almost every*. The cost discussion distinguishes the permissions required by a chosen internal structure from those forced by categorization.
