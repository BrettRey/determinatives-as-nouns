## Proposed repairs (none applied)

### Defects

**R1 (l. 297; needs Brett's analysis).** *CGEL*'s negative *few* vs positive *a few* isn't re-expressed or answered, and it bears directly on putting *few* in Det. Candidate insertion after "peripheral \mention{a} contributes no singular-count requirement.":

> It does contribute orientation: \textit{CGEL} contrasts negative \mention{few}, which as determiner makes its clause negative (\mention{Few people came to the meeting, did they?}), with positive \mention{a few} \citep[393]{huddleston2002}, and on this analysis that contrast is the contribution of peripheral \mention{a}.

The last clause commits the analysis to a claim only Brett can make. If he doesn't hold it, the paragraph needs his account instead. The DECISIONS.md 2026-09-17 ground (1) should then be marked superseded, or restated.

**R2 (l. 738).** The head genitive isn't a rule *CGEL* states separately.
- old: `peripheral attachment in \mention{almost every} and \mention{only you} (§\ref{sec:payne}); and the head genitive in \mention{everyone's} and \mention{Edward's} (§\ref{sec:inflection}).`
- new: `and peripheral attachment in \mention{almost every} and \mention{only you} (§\ref{sec:payne}). \textit{CGEL} already states the head genitive once, by the head noun, with \mention{everyone's} beside \mention{Edward's} (§\ref{sec:inflection}); within Noun that statement holds as written.`
- Reason: pp. 479–480. The repair also puts the paper's point more exactly.

**R3 (l. 245).** The gloss drops the inflection half of Van Eynde's criterion, which l. 247's extrapolation relies on.
- old: `The criterion is inflectional agreement shared with adjectives, and English has none to share, since its adjectives don't agree.`
- new: `The criterion is inflection and agreement shared with prenominal adjectives, and English has no agreement to share, since its adjectives don't agree.`
- Reason: Van Eynde 2003 §§1.1–1.2 ("the same inflectional variation and the same agreement").

**R4 (l. 754), minor verb.**
- old: `where \textit{CGEL} needs conversion for one`
- new: `where \textit{CGEL} uses conversion for one`
- Reason: this is the cooling applied on 18 September to "must treat". *CGEL* records "one or two nouns formed by conversion" as a lexical note, not as a rule it's forced into.
