The repeated “The X...” pattern has distinct jobs here: defining the comparison, reporting its result, introducing a rule, or marking a change of question. “Shared projection...” occurs on either side of a subsection boundary, first to restrict interchangeability and then to introduce modifier selection. These are useful orientations. The question openings introduce live questions, not generic throat-clearing. No CUT or REWRITE is required on cadence grounds.
