# Recommended pass order for *Determinatives as nouns in English*

9 September 2026. This sequence starts from the current manuscript, after the section-by-section revisions, the correction to the local-economy claim, and the introduction–conclusion alignment.

The paper's main remaining risk is an inference that crosses one of its carefully separated distinctions: category and function, nounhood and ordinary headedness, shared coverage and comparative economy, or distributional separation and taxonomic rank. Those distinctions should be checked before further compression. The other demonstrated risk is loss of necessary premises during readability editing. That determines where cutting and continuous reading belong in this sequence.

## Recommended sequence

1. **Terminological hygiene → level/category audit → Lakoffian metaphor.** Establish exactly what each occurrence of *Noun*, *nominal*, *projection*, *Head*, *fusion*, *inheritance*, *boundary* and *economy* means. Check that the prose, tables, rules and trees preserve the same distinctions. The metaphor pass belongs here because containment, inheritance and economy can make an organizational preference seem established before it has been argued. Formal tree terminology is not defective merely because its ordinary-language origin is metaphorical. Syntactic projection must also remain distinct from inductive projectibility.

2. **Source reread and quotation audit, followed by charitable engagement and negative-claims audit.** Check the premises on which the comparison depends: CGEL's pronoun-membership rationale, its noun and fusion analyses, Payne's modifier-domain account, Hudson's hierarchy, and the redistribution alternatives. Use the existing page-verification records and revisit the passages whose use has changed; there is no reason to reread every cited book in full. Then test whether the rivals receive their strongest relevant formulations. Charity must not award fusion an economy advantage merely because it reuses a rule. Check novelty claims and historical generalizations against the actual sources in this same round.

3. **Measurement construction → estimand alignment → statistical inference → numbers.** Treat this as one connected examination of §5 and Appendix A. Start with what the coded objects are, then ask what the reported quantities describe, what the comparisons warrant, and whether the text matches the outputs. Specific checks include the 65 Det–Head instances versus ordinary arguments; public versus recovered matrices; analysis-coded features; dependence among word forms; initialization and feature sensitivity; and best-objective versus best-agreement clustering. The existing independent reproductions justify reusing verified outputs. Recompute if a discrepancy, changed input or untested assumption makes it necessary. The absence of a population estimand is a limit to preserve, not a reason to overlook the empirical claims.

4. **Projectibility/warrant audit, including an explicit comparison of the four accounts and the substantive figure audit.** With terminology and evidence settled, test each move from a construction to a taxonomic preference. Identify what the comparison supports, which assumptions and lexical permissions are held fixed, what each alternative shares, and what would favour a different organization. Trace the same examples through the prose, Figures 1–6, the comparison tables and §8.2. Check the positive argument for inheritance as carefully as the qualifications. This is also the place for the unearned-inference check exemplified by the corrected local-economy sentence. Apply the projectibility pass to the scope and warrant actually claimed; do not import an HPC ontology, a causal mechanism or a new predictive study into this descriptive paper.

5. **Contribution alignment.** Now check title, abstract, keywords, opening, headings and conclusion against the argument that survived the preceding checks. The introduction and conclusion have just been aligned, so the abstract is the main remaining exposure: it still says that distributional differences warrant distinct *subcategories*, where the revised conclusion separates support for a distinction from the argument about rank. Preserve the principal inclusion claim unless the argument audit supplies a substantive reason to change it. This is a focused alignment check, not another restructuring exercise.

6. **Editorial scar tissue, then redundancy.** These are separate judgments: a response to an obsolete objection may be dispensable even when unique, whereas repeated wording may perform distinct necessary jobs. Protect the pronoun-membership premise, the article membership test, the strongest separate-D alternative, the distinction between an article's use permission and a common noun's determination requirement, and the limits of the empirical evidence. The earlier cutting round removed substantive support; that is a concrete reason to demand an account of each proposed cut's argumentative function. Repetition between the introduction and conclusion is warranted when it moves from a question to an answer. Do not combine this cutting round with density leavening.

7. **Continuous reader pass, followed by coherence/cohesion repair.** Read the resulting manuscript in order, without explanations supplied from the conversation or review history. This is the check that the preceding cuts have left a recoverable argument. Prior section-by-section editing does not establish that the joins work for a new reader. Concentrate on the changes of question: what counts as a noun, how independent expressions are structured, what the empirical record establishes, where determinatives sit in the hierarchy, and what the fragment contributes. Repair observed failures rather than reworking every transition.

8. **Reader-guided density leavening and figure planning.** Add an intermediate step, unpack a phrase, or improve an existing visual only where the preceding reading identified a burden. §8.2's rules and the compound comparison are the obvious places to watch. The manuscript already has six figures containing eleven trees and seven tables; figure planning should assess this existing explanatory provision before proposing additions. The early figure audit tested correctness; this pass asks whether another representation would help the reader. Verify any changed diagrams afterwards. Keep this round separate from redundancy so that necessary explanation is not immediately cut again.

9. **Adversarial opening read → review board → objection triage.** The opening read tests whether readers identify the problem and contribution from the title, abstract and first two pages alone. The board tests whether the full argument delivers. Both matter here: earlier external reviews found the question inaccessible, while subsequent expert reviews prompted substantial changes to the comparative argument. Use independent readers and choose models when this round is commissioned, following the project's dispatch rule. Include the strongest separate-D ordinary-Head objection, the fusion/modifier-domain objection, and a reader outside the CGEL framework. The question is whether the organizational preference is justified on its stated terms; do not impose unique empirical coverage or minimum description length as an unstated acceptance criterion. Triage substantive findings before polishing. A repair that changes a premise returns to the relevant earlier check, rather than restarting every pass.

10. **Rhetoric and humour → paragraph openings → de-AI prose.** At this point, improve how the settled argument is carried. Preserve useful parallelism, especially the three-part membership test and its matching application. Check whether antithesis implies a stronger contrast than the accounts support. Assess existing wit without requiring jokes. Adjust paragraph openings after the paragraph sequence is stable. The de-AI pass comes last in this group so it cannot remove deliberate parallelism or necessary qualifications simply because they resemble a common stylistic pattern. Recheck passages newly created by board revisions for editorial scar tissue.

11. **Bibliography integrity → any needed central bibliography merge → house style → full build/render → proofreading.** These checks should operate on settled content. Recent successful XeLaTeX runs are useful evidence, but they are not the complete build-integrity or visual-proofing passes. Check all tree branches, table placement, captions, references and the existing small overfull lines in the rendered PDF. Proofreading should read the final prose, not a version immediately superseded by argument edits. Rebuild and inspect pages affected by corrections.

12. **Submission package, when submission is intended.** Apply the chosen venue's blinding, metadata, disclosure and supplementary-file requirements to the settled manuscript. This stage is deferred because the current task is preparation of a working paper, not submission. Recheck opening alignment if venue-specific changes alter the promise made to readers.

## Work to reuse, and the justification

- **External-review triage:** consult the existing rejection triage and review-board synthesis as the initial objection checklist. A fresh full triage of the same reports would duplicate completed work. After stage 9, triage the new findings and verify closure of any still-relevant older objections. Earlier triage recommendations that elevated coordination to the principal novelty have been superseded by the author's explicit hierarchy of claims.
- **Corpus and numerical reproduction:** independent reruns and a separate numerical check already exist. The first empirical task is to check interpretation and continued correspondence with those outputs, rather than rerunning unchanged analyses merely because the ledger says “never run.” Reopen computation for a concrete reason.
- **Structure and cohesion:** the just-completed revision is substantial prior work. Retain its section order as the working arrangement; the later continuous reading tests it rather than presuming it needs replacing.
- **Projectibility and metaphor:** retain their inferential checks. Their relevance follows from the paper's category argument and its language of inheritance, projection, boundaries and economy. Homeostasis, causal maintenance and experimental preregistration are not claims this paper makes, so those branches of the generic procedures do not require new manuscript content.
- **Rhetoric and humour:** retain the rhetoric check because this paper depends on parallel comparisons. If the settled text contains no humour, report that limited finding; adding humour is not a completion requirement.
- **Ledger status:** use artifacts and the current text to judge what remains. The board currently misidentifies the manuscript as having no figures or coded measurements and does not recognize several completed reviews. Those detector results do not justify skipping the corresponding checks or duplicating the documented work.

No whole pass needs to be permanently omitted. The savings come from reusing established work, combining compatible audits, applying irrelevant branches only where their premises hold, and preventing rounds of cutting and expansion from cancelling one another.
