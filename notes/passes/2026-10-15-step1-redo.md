## 2. The schema stated by a property
**Support.**

- The property "carries its own description of the individuals" was proposed on 19 September (P2). It predicted that personal pronouns would have the secondary use, which the paper then left open, and the 4 and 15 October searches confirmed it (_a he or a she_, _mes_, _the real me_).

- It also excludes the articles and _every_ for a reason, not by listing them.

- It answers the open question about _the few_ and _the many_ from the property itself. _Few_, _many_, and _some_ describe a quantity, not the individuals. That's why they keep their quantificational force, where the secondary use loses it (§6.1: "\mention{an anybody} is a person of no particular standing"); _the lucky few_ is a small number of the lucky. So they're the independent use with _the_ in Det, which §4.3 analyses, not this one.

- §5.5 says the secondary use and the former complex determinatives "weren't among the diagnostics of §2; they're additional consequences, not tests set in advance". 2b's "predicts the pronoun input" is a prediction of the schema, not of the grouping, so the two sentences don't conflict.

- GLM-5.3 proposed counting them as vague cardinalities. That would put them in the rule, but they don't take its output: plural inflection, or "groups of the cardinal's size".
