## Routing judgment

The reader's question is why English determinatives should be included in Noun despite their distinctive syntax. The paper's answer is that their nominal constructional profile can be organized through the membership rationale already used for pronouns, with lexical and constructional restrictions retained. Its explanatory sequence is intact. §7.2 has the highest processing burden, but named variables, the permissions table and concrete applications make it recoverable. **Clarity repair**, not reconstruction, is appropriate. A fresh reader's inability to distinguish the three commitments or to explain the strongest separate-D alternative would challenge this judgment.
