## The question asked: *a few mistakes*

"In *a few mistakes*, *few* heads the NP in Det, while *a* modifies the larger NP headed by *mistakes*" (l. 277) assigns each function at the level the paper's own machinery requires. *Few* is the ultimate lexical head (l. 327) of the NP filling Det; *a* is a peripheral modifier whose Head is the NP *few mistakes* (third structural condition, l. 640; "the modifiers precede any determiner belonging to the NP they modify", l. 486). The same allocation recurs at l. 113, 295, 307, 615, 661, matches Table 4's note (Mod column internal only; peripheral *a* has a separate permission), and matches `DECISIONS.md` 2026-09-19. No sentence assigns Det to *a* or to *a few* in the dependent use. The residues of the superseded 17 September analysis are D3 and D10 below.

One consistency question is not a wording defect and is left to Brett (see "Question for Brett").

## Defects

**D1. l. 29 (abstract): wrong fusion type for the second example.**

New: "I replace the grammar's fusion of Head with a determiner or modifier function by ordinary Head relations"

**D2. l. 29 and l. 740: the taxonomy credited with a structural reanalysis.**
l. 29 old: "The grouping also unifies three secondary uses the grammar states separately and reanalyses its complex determinatives"
l. 29 new: "The grouping also unifies three secondary uses the grammar states separately, and peripheral and predeterminer modification replace its complex determinatives"
l. 740 old: "(§\ref{sec:complex-determinatives}). These generalizations are available under either ordinary-Head taxonomy: the proposed account states their domain through Noun membership"
l. 740 new: "(§\ref{sec:complex-determinatives}), a reanalysis that doesn't invoke Noun membership. The generalizations of the previous paragraph are available under either ordinary-Head taxonomy: the proposed account states their domain through Noun membership"
Reason: §3.2's reanalysis uses peripheral and predeterminer functions only; nothing in l. 293–311 depends on Noun membership or a nominal feature, and l. 740 itself says the result is available under either taxonomy. As written, the abstract makes it a product of the grouping, and l. 740 gives it a "domain through Noun membership" it doesn't have. (Related: l. 734 lists "the former complex determinatives" among "additional consequences" inside the case for Noun membership; the repair at l. 740 keeps that reading from implying support for the taxonomy.)
