## Bottom line

The diagnosis is sound and the section-level scores are plausible: §5 is the bottleneck, §§2–3 read well, and the introduction front-loads framework vocabulary. Two of its seven remedies are worth doing now, one is worth doing at a quarter of the proposed size, and one asks for something the paper already did.

The premise is stale in one respect that matters. **The review measures the paper against an *ELL* reader; *ELL* rejected it on 7 September and `STATUS.md` records `venue: none`.** How much CGEL the reader is assumed to know is a venue question: a CGEL-adjacent venue wants §5's precision, a general syntax venue wants the primer. The portfolio rule puts a venue decision record before major retargeting work, so the large items here (a rebuilt §5, a framework primer sized for non-CGEL readers) shouldn't start before that decision.

Nothing in this review bears on the open substantive items (polarity, *CGEL* p. 421, independent *every*). An accessibility review isn't a soundness review, and it shouldn't reorder the punch list ahead of them.
