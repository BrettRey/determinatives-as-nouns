## Items not to misclassify as lost April arguments

*Or so* is absent from the current source, but it was **not in the 29 April draft**. It was added in July and was cut in the latest revision at Brett's explicit request because its scalar/idiomatic character prevented it from supplying independent category evidence. It is a later addition-and-removal, not an April omission.

The missing *few/either/neither* corpus attestations are also not lost April evidence. April did not have this corpus inventory. The current version reports that gap rather than filling it with constructed examples.
